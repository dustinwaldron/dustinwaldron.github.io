Design Patterns: Why They Matter and How SOLID Principles Give Them Teeth

Design Patterns Are Not a Fancy Acronym. They Are a Professional Discipline. When I review code, the difference between a team that is shipping features and a team that is building durable systems is usually not raw intelligence. It is the discipline of design. The best engineering teams do not treat design as a luxury. They treat it as a force multiplier. Design patterns are one of the clearest expressions of that discipline. They are not “pattern matching” for the sake of aesthetics. They are battle-tested ways to structure code so that software is easier to understand, easier to extend, and less likely to collapse under the weight of change. ...
